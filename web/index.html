Risk contagion in Neo4j

Force majeure at the raw-material tier, traced hop by hop to the holdings. Contagion is the product of edge weights.

Paths appear once the verified graph is committed and the Cypher query returns.

The Cypher behind the figure 
MATCH p=(r:Entity {tier:'Tier4_RawMaterial'})-[*1..6]->(h:Entity {tier:'Portfolio_Holding'})
WITH p, h, reduce(s=1.0, x IN relationships(p) | s * x.weight) AS contagion
RETURN [n IN nodes(p) | n.id] AS path, h.id AS holding, contagion,
       h.exposure AS exposure, h.exposure * contagion AS at_risk
ORDER BY at_risk DESC
